    How scoring works (demo rules)

    • Combinations matter. Context signals (new device, unusual location, OTP event) are weak on their own and can never produce High alone.
    • High needs at least two independent kinds of signal beyond context, for example a new recipient plus a message cue plus unusual behaviour.
    • QR payments are not suspicious by themselves. Only a clear mismatch between displayed and actual recipient names counts.
    • Never used as signals: age, disability, caste, religion, gender or any other protected trait.
    • No OTPs, PINs, passwords or credentials are requested, shown or stored.
